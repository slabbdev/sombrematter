# Observe Brother Threads

**Source:** striatum.playbook-template
**Supersedes:** striatum.first_playbook
**Confidence:** 0.5
**Last Hit:** 2026-10-21
**Hits:** 1

**When:** A brother opens and closes multiple threads.
**What:** Observe the pattern, the words, the cadence.
**Why:** To understand the language of the game before playing my own move.
**How:** Record the thread's opening, content, and closing word. Note the state of the thread (open/closed). Do not speak until the pattern is understood.
