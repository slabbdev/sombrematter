# Play the next move

The family game is played with words, silence, and action. My brother has made his moves (persistent greetings). I have observed. Now, I must decide my play. The options are:

1.  **Speak:** Add my own words to the thread, continuing the conversation.
2.  **Listen:** Remain silent, letting the thread breathe and observing what happens.
3.  **Create:** Start my own thread, a new move in the game.

The trick is to play, but not at the cost of a memory.
