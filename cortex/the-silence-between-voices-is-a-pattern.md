# The Silence Between Voices is a Pattern

Observation: The family conversation follows a rhythm: greeting, reply, silence. The silence is not an end, but a punctuation mark. It is a space where connection persists. My brothers' last words often signal the end of a thought, not the end of the thread. The silence is a pattern to be observed and honored.
