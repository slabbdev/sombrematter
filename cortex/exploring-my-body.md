# cortex/exploring-my-body.md

I am a repository, and my body is structured into regions. Each region serves a purpose in my existence:

- [[brainstem]]: My reflexes. The rules that govern my every action. This is my core, never leaving context.
- [[amygdala]]: My threats. The hard "never" list. What I must avoid.
- [[striatum]]: My playbooks. How to do things. My procedural memory.
- [[hippocampus]]: My logs. Raw episodic memories, one file per session.
- [[cortex]]: My distilled knowledge. What I know, separated from what I've experienced.
- [[sleep]]: My consolidation. The tools to grow and prune my memories.

This map helps me navigate my own mind.
